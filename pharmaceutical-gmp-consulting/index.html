Regulatory Context

Engineering that holds up to a Health Canada inspection.

Drug manufacturers in Canada operate under the Food and Drug Regulations, Part C, Division 2, and Health Canada’s Good Manufacturing Practices guide for drug products. Many Ontario sites also supply the United States, which brings FDA 21 CFR Parts 210 and 211, and Part 11 for electronic records, into the same project.

Our pharmaceutical consulting work sits where those requirements meet the production floor. We write and execute the IQ, OQ and PQ protocols, qualify equipment moved between lines or sites, validate the computerized systems that control it, and keep facility documentation current, so your quality unit has evidence it can approve and an inspector can follow.

We work as an extension of your engineering and quality teams rather than at arm’s length, whether the job is a single piece of equipment or a full packaging line.

Pharmaceutical Services

GMP consulting services for drug manufacturers.

Each service can be scoped as a fixed deliverable or as embedded support on a capital project.

Equipment Qualification (IQ/OQ/PQ)

Risk-based qualification protocols, execution, and summary reports for new and modified process and packaging equipment.

  • User requirements & design qualification
  • IQ, OQ & PQ protocol development and execution
  • Deviation handling & final summary reports

Transfer Qualification (TQ)

Requalification of equipment relocated between rooms, lines, or sites, scoped to what actually changed.

  • Change impact & requalification assessment
  • TQ protocols tied to the original qualification
  • Updated as-built and equipment records

Packaging Line Qualification

Bottling and packaging lines qualified as an integrated system, whatever equipment the line is built from.

  • Counting, filling, capping & induction sealing
  • Labeling, serialization & Track & Trace
  • Line clearance and changeover verification

Computer System Validation

CSV for PLC, HMI, SCADA and packaging control systems, with a data-integrity focus.

  • GAMP 5 risk-based validation approach
  • 21 CFR Part 11 & EU Annex 11 assessments
  • Audit trail, access & data integrity review

Facility Engineering & As-Builts

GMP facility layouts, expansions, and the as-built documentation that keeps them inspection-ready.

  • Personnel, material & waste flow
  • Room classification & HVAC requirements
  • As-built drawings & facility records

Inspection Readiness & QMS

Gap assessments and remediation ahead of Health Canada or FDA inspections.

  • GMP gap assessments & mock inspections
  • Validation master plans & SOPs
  • CAPA & deviation support
