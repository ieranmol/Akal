Privacy

Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect personal information on akaleg.com and when you contact us.

Who we are

AKAL Engineering Group Ltd.

This policy explains how AKAL Engineering Group Ltd. ("AKAL", "we") collects, uses, and protects personal information through akaleg.com and when you contact us. We are based in London, Ontario, Canada, and we follow Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and, for people in Quebec, Quebec's Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector.

Person in charge of personal information: Privacy Officer, AKAL Engineering Group Ltd., London, Ontario, Canada. Email info@akaleg.com or call +1 519-504-2721.

What we collect

Information we collect and why

  • Get in Touch form: your name, company, work email, phone number (optional), industry, service needed, project stage, timeline, and your message. We use this only to respond to your enquiry, scope and quote the work, and manage our relationship with you if you become a client.
  • Email and phone: the details you give us when you contact us directly, used for the same purposes.
  • Website analytics, only if you accept: Google Analytics records which pages are viewed, how visitors arrive, and general device, browser, and approximate location details. It does not give us your name or contact details, and we do not use it for advertising.

We will not add you to a mailing list or send you marketing emails without your permission, and we do not sell personal information.

Cookies

Cookies and your choice

Analytics cookies are off by default. They are only set if you choose Accept in the cookie banner. Your choice is saved in your browser so we do not ask again on every page.

  • _ga and _ga_* (Google Analytics): distinguish visitors and sessions. Kept for up to 2 years. Set only after you accept.
  • akal-consent (stored in your browser, not a cookie): remembers whether you accepted or declined.

You can change or withdraw your choice at any time with Cookie Settings, which is also in the footer of every page.

Service providers

Who handles your information for us

We use a few trusted service providers to run this website and our communications. They handle personal information on our behalf and only to provide their service:

  • Google stores Get in Touch form submissions (Google Apps Script and Google Sheets) and, if you accept cookies, provides Google Analytics.
  • Apple iCloud Mail delivers and stores email sent to our @akaleg.com addresses, including form notifications.
  • GitHub hosts this website and may log technical data such as IP addresses for security.

These providers may store information outside Canada and Quebec, including in the United States, where it may be accessible to authorities under local law. We choose providers with appropriate safeguards and share only what each service needs.

Retention & security

How long we keep it and how we protect it

We keep Get in Touch submissions and related correspondence for 2 years from our last contact with you, then delete them. If you become a client, we keep project records for as long as our contract and legal obligations require. Google Analytics data is kept for no longer than 14 months.

We limit access to the people who need it, keep it in password-protected business accounts, and will notify you and the relevant privacy authority if a breach creates a real risk of serious harm.

Your rights

Access, correction, and withdrawal

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, correct it, have it deleted, or withdraw your consent, subject to legal limits. Contact our Privacy Officer at info@akaleg.com. We respond within 30 days.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada or, in Quebec, the Commission d'accès à l'information.
