Why It Matters

Food safety depends on how the line is built.

Food and beverage manufacturers in Canada work under food safety requirements that expect hazards to be controlled, equipment to be suitable and cleanable, and processes to be documented. Many also answer to customer audits and third-party certification schemes that ask for the same evidence in more detail.

Much of that evidence comes from engineering decisions: how equipment is specified, how lines are laid out, how product, people, and waste move through the building, and whether cleaning and changeover can be done reliably at production speed.

We bring pharmaceutical-grade qualification discipline to food and beverage operations, scaled to the risk of your products and the size of your site.
