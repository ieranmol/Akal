About AKAL

We work as part of your team, not at arm’s length.

A London, Ontario GMP engineering and validation consultancy, built for the realities of regulated manufacturing in Canada.

Our Story

Formed around regulated manufacturing, not away from it.

AKAL Engineering was founded to close a specific gap: regulated manufacturers need engineering partners who understand both the compliance requirements and the operational pressure of running a production line. We built our practice around that intersection.

From our base in London, Ontario, we work with pharmaceutical, nutraceutical, and food & beverage manufacturers across North America, supporting projects from early facility concepts through commissioned, validated, running equipment.

Project Spotlight · Ontario · 2022–2023

Pharmaceutical bottling and packaging line qualification.

For a pharmaceutical manufacturer in Ontario, AKAL qualified a solid-dose bottling and packaging line. Our engineers developed and executed the performance qualification protocol and the transfer qualification protocols for the equipment on the line, and delivered the supporting documentation for quality approval.

The same approach applies to any process, packaging, or utility equipment: the work combined equipment-level qualification with line-level performance testing, so the client’s quality unit could release the line on evidence that reflected real production conditions.
