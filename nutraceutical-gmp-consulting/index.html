Regulatory Context

GMP for natural health products is a site-wide requirement.

In Canada, most nutraceuticals are regulated as natural health products under the Natural Health Products Regulations. Sites that manufacture, package, label, or import NHPs need a site licence and must meet the GMP requirements set out in Part 3 of the Regulations, and Health Canada has been increasing its GMP compliance monitoring of NHP sites.

Meeting those expectations is as much an engineering problem as a paperwork one. Equipment has to be specified, installed, and qualified correctly. Cleaning has to be shown to work. Material and personnel flow has to make sense, and labeling and packaging lines have to run reliably at speed.

Our nutraceutical GMP consulting brings pharmaceutical-grade qualification discipline to NHP operations, scaled to the size of your site and the risk of your products.
